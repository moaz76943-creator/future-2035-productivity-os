# Store listing — draft (claims limited to implemented features)

**App name:** Deep Focus

**Short description (en, ≤80):** Tasks, focus timer, calendar, notes and flashcards in one private app.

**Short description (ar):** المهام والتركيز والتقويم والملاحظات والبطاقات في تطبيق واحد خاص بك.

**Full description (en):**
Deep Focus brings your planning, focus and learning together — on your device, without an account.

• Tasks with projects, subtasks, priorities, due dates, reminders and repeats
• Focus timer: Pomodoro, custom timer and stopwatch (with a break of a fifth of your focus time), linked to the task you are working on
• Immersive full-screen clock over a dim nature photo or pure black, and flip the phone face down to start
• Distraction guard (optional): a calm 5-second breathing pause or a strict block for the apps you choose, during focus or on a schedule, and Shorts and Reels closed
• Nature sound mixer with per-sound volume, saved mixes and a gently fading sleep timer
• Calendar with day, week, month and agenda views, recurring and all-day events
• Notes with Markdown, checklists, tags, notebooks and links to projects and tasks
• Flashcards with spaced repetition (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) and read-aloud
• Goals and habits with streaks
• Insights built only from your own records
• Global search across everything
• 8 languages including Arabic with full right-to-left layout
• Private by design: encrypted on-device storage, no account; password-encrypted backups. One banner ad (Google AdMob, with Google's consent form where required)
• Optional AI summaries with your own provider, only after you consent
